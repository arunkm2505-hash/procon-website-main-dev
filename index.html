About Procon Infra LLP

Engineering and execution, built around one accountable team.

Procon Infra LLP is an integrated Engineering, Procurement and Construction company delivering industrial and infrastructure projects. Our delivery model brings engineering, procurement, construction and project controls together to provide coordinated execution from design development through testing, commissioning and handover.

  • Integrated EPC delivery across multiple disciplines
  • Engineering-led planning and execution
  • One accountable team from design to handover
