About Procon Infra LLP

Engineering and execution, built around one accountable team.

Procon Infra LLP is an integrated Engineering, Procurement and Construction company delivering industrial and infrastructure projects across civil infrastructure, PEB and structural steel, composite construction and MEP services.

We combine engineering-led planning with procurement, construction and project controls to provide clients with a coordinated delivery model from project definition through commissioning and handover.

Our industrial ecosystem

Built on an established industrial ecosystem

Backed by the experience of Royal Fab Group.

Procon is part of the Royal Fab Group, an established industrial group with a legacy dating back to 1989.

The wider group brings together engineering, manufacturing and execution capabilities, strengthening Procon's ability to coordinate complex industrial projects from engineering and procurement through fabrication and site execution.

Where appropriate to project scope, Procon can leverage the group's structural steel and PEB manufacturing capabilities to create closer coordination between design, fabrication and construction.
