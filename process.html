The nine-stage methodology

From kick-off to handover

01

Kick-off & Inputs

Project kick-off meeting, understanding scope, objectives and deliverables.

  • Collection of drawings, data and site information
  • Resource planning & mobilization plan
02

Design & Approvals

Engineering coordination, civil, structural & MEP design integration.

  • GA & fabrication drawings, authority coordination
  • Value engineering & constructability review
03

Procurement

Vendor & material planning, purchase orders and expediting.

  • Inspection & QA of incoming materials
  • Logistics & on-time delivery management
04

Fabrication / Civil Works

Shop fabrication as per drawings and standards; quality control at every stage.

  • Civil works including foundations, flooring, roads
05

Site Execution

Safe, systematic erection; civil, structural & MEP execution.

  • Alignment, bolting, welding & grouting
  • Daily progress monitoring
06

MEP Installation & Finishes

HVAC, electrical, plumbing, fire protection installation, testing and pre-commissioning.

  • Finishes and touch-ups, quality checks at all levels
07

Testing & Snag Closure

Functional testing of all systems, snag identification and rectification.

  • QA/QC inspections, client walkthrough & sign-off
08

Commissioning & O&M Training

System commissioning, training for operation and maintenance teams.

  • Submission of manuals & compliance documents
09

Handover

Final documentation submission, as-built drawings and dossiers, project handover and close-out.

One process. One team. One commitment. Delivering excellence, every time.
