Composite construction

Composite construction designed for efficient industrial delivery.

Our composite construction solutions combine steel and concrete systems to optimise structural performance, construction speed and coordination across the project.

How composite action works

Concrete slab, shear studs, metal deck, steel beam

Shear studs welded to the top flange lock the concrete slab and steel beam into one composite section, so the two materials share load as a single stronger unit rather than acting independently.

Composite construction in practice

Box girder fabrication with shear studs

Composite action through shear studs: headed studs, 20mm diameter, 100mm height, IS 2062 / ASTM A108 material, welded using capacitor discharge stud welding.

Fabrication process flow

1

Plate Cutting

2

Fit-up & Tack Welding

3

Internal Diaphragm & Stiffener Welding

4

Submerged Arc Welding (SAW)

5

Shear Stud Welding

6

Final Inspection & Camber Check

Key benefits

  • Composite action between steel girder and concrete slab
  • Higher load carrying capacity
  • Improved stiffness and reduced deflection
  • Efficient use of material
  • Enhanced structural performance
